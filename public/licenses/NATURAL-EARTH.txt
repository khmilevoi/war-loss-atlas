The land file is a decorative geographic background. The country source supplies LABEL_Y/LABEL_X representative label coordinates and modern admin-0 boundary linework on the decorative basemap. These boundaries do not reconstruct territorial control for the selected year. LABEL_Y/LABEL_X are rounded to 0.5 degrees in a frozen alias dictionary. Only unambiguous exact current-name matches are used; historical, disputed, multi-country and unmatched cases remain explicitly unplaced pending theater review. Source data may contain inaccuracies. No coordinate is a casualty location, no boundary or sovereignty claim is made, and no country death totals are calculated.

The pinned GeoJSON is identified by commit and hash, not assumed identical to the version 4.0.0 ZIP described on Natural Earth's land download page.
